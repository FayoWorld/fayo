1. Introduction

FAYO — Future Assets You Own — is a community token on the Solana blockchain built around the future of AI and digital ownership.

The token was created with no mint authority, no freeze authority, and immutable metadata, which makes its supply structure fixed, trustless, and irreversible. The exact scripts used to create it are published in the project's public GitHub repository.

FAYO does not represent ownership, revenue rights, or investment guarantees. It is a freely transferable community token within the Solana ecosystem.

5. Vesting

  • All locked allocations are held in 7 Streamflow vesting contracts that are non-cancelable and non-transferable.
  • Vesting started Oct 30, 2026 (~11:20 UTC) with no cliff and linear monthly (every 30 days) unlocks.
  • Creator (20%) vests over 24 months.
  • Exchange Listings (16%), Liquidity Reserve (10%), Advertising & Marketing (10%), Airdrop (10%), Support & Development Team (10%) and Services & Providers (1%) vest over 12 months.
  • Only Initial Liquidity (10%) and Liquidity Management (5%) are unlocked. Staking Rewards (8%) are locked in the Streamflow staking reward pool.

At the fixed rate this is roughly 27% in total for a 6-month lock (1x weight) and roughly 110% annualized for a 12-month lock (2x weight), only while the reward fund lasts.

Rewards are paid from a finite fund. When it is exhausted, rewards stop. The reward rate is a mechanical parameter of the pool, not a promise of returns, and nothing about the price or value of FAYO is guaranteed.
