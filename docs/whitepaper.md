## 1. Introduction

**FAYO** — *Future Assets You Own* — is a community token on the Solana blockchain built around the future of AI and digital ownership.

The token was created with **no mint authority, no freeze authority, and immutable metadata**, which makes its supply structure fixed, trustless, and irreversible. The exact scripts used to create it are published in the project's public GitHub repository.

FAYO does not represent ownership, revenue rights, or investment guarantees. It is a freely transferable community token within the Solana ecosystem.

> *The Future Is Yours.*
